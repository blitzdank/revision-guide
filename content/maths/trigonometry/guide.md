## Right-angled triangles: SOHCAHTOA

**These ratios are given on the AQA formula sheet** — you don't need to memorise them, but you must know how to use them:

$$\sin A = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}} \qquad \cos A = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}} \qquad \tan A = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

**Finding a side:** identify which two sides are involved, pick the matching ratio, rearrange.

**Example:** a right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of 10 cm and one angle of 35°. Find the opposite side.

$$\sin(35°) = \frac{\text{opposite}}{10} \implies \text{opposite} = 10 \times \sin(35°) = 10 \times 0.5736 = 5.74 \text{ cm (3 s.f.)}$$

**Finding an angle:** rearrange using the inverse function ($\sin^{-1}$, $\cos^{-1}$, $\tan^{-1}$).

**Example:** a right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 6 cm and an adjacent side of 8 cm. Find the angle.

$$\tan(\theta) = \frac{6}{8} = 0.75 \implies \theta = \tan^{-1}(0.75) = 36.9° \text{ (1 d.p.)}$$

> **Exam tip:** Always label the sides (opposite/adjacent/hypotenuse) relative to the angle you're working with first — the same side can be "opposite" for one angle and "adjacent" for another in the same triangle.

## Non-right-angled triangles

For triangles **without** a right angle, you need the **sine rule** or **cosine rule** — **both are given on the formula sheet**.

### Sine rule

$$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$$

Use when you know a side and its opposite angle, plus one more side or angle.

**Example:** in a triangle, $a=8$, $A=40°$, $B=65°$. Find $b$.

$$\frac{8}{\sin 40°} = \frac{b}{\sin 65°} \implies b = \frac{8 \times \sin 65°}{\sin 40°} = \frac{8 \times 0.9063}{0.6428} = 11.28 \text{ (2 d.p.)}$$

### Cosine rule

$$a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$$

Use when you know all three sides (to find an angle — rearrange first) or two sides and the angle between them (to find the third side).

**Example:** a triangle has sides $b=7$, $c=9$, with an angle $A=60°$ between them. Find $a$.

$$a^2 = 7^2+9^2 - 2(7)(9)\cos(60°) = 49+81-126(0.5) = 130-63=67$$
$$a = \sqrt{67} = 8.19 \text{ (3 s.f.)}$$

### Area of any triangle

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$$

Use when you know two sides and the angle between them. **Also given on the formula sheet.**

> **Exam tip:** Before choosing a method, sketch the triangle and label what you know — it makes it obvious whether you need SOHCAHTOA (right angle present), the sine rule (angle/opposite-side pair known), or the cosine rule (three sides, or two sides and the included angle).
